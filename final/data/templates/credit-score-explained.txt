UNDERSTANDING YOUR CREDIT SCORE
FinSmart – Money Confidence for Students
==================================================

WHAT IS A CREDIT SCORE?
A 3-digit number (300–850) that tells lenders how
reliable you are at repaying borrowed money.

SCORE RANGES
  800–850  Excellent
  740–799  Very Good
  670–739  Good
  580–669  Fair
  300–579  Poor

FIVE FACTORS THAT BUILD YOUR SCORE
  1. Payment History        35%
  2. Amounts Owed            30%
  3. Length of History       15%
  4. New Credit              10%
  5. Credit Mix              10%

STUDENT ACTION PLAN
  [ ] Get a student credit card
  [ ] Use it for ONE small recurring bill
  [ ] Pay the FULL statement balance monthly
  [ ] Keep credit utilization under 30%
  [ ] Check your free report annually
  [ ] Never cosign for friends

WARNINGS
  ✗ Missing a payment can drop your score 60+ points
  ✗ Closing old cards shortens your history
  ✗ Applying for many cards at once hurts
