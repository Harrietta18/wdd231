BUILDING A GOOD MONEY REPUTATION
FinSmart - Money Confidence for Nigerian Students
==================================================

WHY IT MATTERS
A good money reputation helps people and institutions trust you.
It can affect borrowing, rent, business partnerships, and support from cooperatives or family networks.

WHAT BUILDS TRUST
  1. Pay bills and loans on time.
  2. Keep records of what you borrow and repay.
  3. Avoid borrowing for wants.
  4. Communicate early if repayment will be late.
  5. Do not guarantee another person's loan unless you can pay it yourself.

STUDENT ACTION PLAN
  [ ] Track every loan or informal debt.
  [ ] Record due dates in your calendar.
  [ ] Keep screenshots or receipts of payments.
  [ ] Separate school money from spending money.
  [ ] Borrow only when there is a repayment plan.

WARNINGS
  - Late repayment can damage trust quickly.
  - Borrowing from many apps or people at once can become overwhelming.
  - A loan used for lifestyle spending usually creates stress later.
