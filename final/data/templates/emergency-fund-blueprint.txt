EMERGENCY FUND BLUEPRINT
FinSmart - Money Confidence for Nigerian Students
==================================================

WHY YOU NEED ONE
A surprise expense should not immediately become debt.
Even a small fund can cover urgent transport, health needs, phone repair, or school materials.

SIZING YOUR FUND
  Beginner:  Naira 50,000
  Starter:   Naira 100,000
  Solid:     1 month of basic expenses
  Strong:    3 months of basic expenses

WHERE TO KEEP IT
  - A separate savings account or wallet.
  - Easy to access within 1 or 2 days.
  - Not mixed with daily spending.
  - Not in risky investments.
  - Not locked if you may need it quickly.

BUILD IT FAST
  1. Save immediately when money enters your account.
  2. Direct gifts, refunds, and extra income to the fund.
  3. Sell one unused item.
  4. Reduce one repeated expense for a month.
  5. Review your goal every semester.
