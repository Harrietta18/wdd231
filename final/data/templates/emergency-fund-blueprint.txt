EMERGENCY FUND BLUEPRINT
FinSmart – Money Confidence for Students
==================================================

WHY YOU NEED ONE
A surprise expense shouldn't turn into debt. Even
a small fund covers 90% of common student emergencies.

SIZING YOUR FUND
  Beginner:       ₦200,000  (covers most surprises)
  Starter:        ₦500,000  (recommended first goal)
  Solid:      1 month of expenses
  Strong:     3 months of expenses
  Bulletproof: 6 months of expenses

WHERE TO KEEP IT
  ✓ High-yield savings account (separate from checking)
  ✓ Easy to access within 1–2 business days
  ✗ Not in investments that can lose value
  ✗ Not in a locked fixed deposit

BUILD IT FAST
  1. Automate a transfer on payday
  2. Direct all windfalls (gifts, refunds) to the fund
  3. Sell one unused item per month
  4. Pick up one extra shift per month
  5. Re-evaluate your fund size every 6 months
