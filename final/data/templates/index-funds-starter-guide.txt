INVESTING FOR BEGINNERS IN NIGERIA
FinSmart - Money Confidence for Nigerian Students
==================================================

START WITH SAFETY
Before investing, build a starter emergency fund and avoid using school fees, rent, or borrowed money.

QUESTIONS TO ASK BEFORE INVESTING
  1. What exactly am I buying?
  2. Can the value go down?
  3. What fees will I pay?
  4. Is the platform or operator regulated?
  5. How long can I leave the money invested?

BEGINNER OPTIONS TO RESEARCH
  - Money market funds.
  - Treasury bills.
  - Mutual funds.
  - Exchange-traded funds.
  - Shares listed on the Nigerian Exchange.

WARNING SIGNS
  - Guaranteed high returns.
  - Pressure to invite friends.
  - No clear regulator or office.
  - Promises that there is no risk.
  - Refusal to explain how profit is made.

COMPOUND GROWTH EXAMPLE
Saving or investing small amounts regularly can grow over time.
The earlier you start, the more time your money has to work.
