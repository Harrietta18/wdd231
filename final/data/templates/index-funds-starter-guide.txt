WHAT IS AN INDEX FUND?
A mutual fund or ETF that tracks a broad market
index (e.g., S&P 500, NSE All-Share, NGX 30).

WHY INDEX FUNDS WIN
  ✦ Low fees (often < 0.10% expense ratio)
  ✦ Diversified in one purchase
  ✦ Outperform most actively managed funds long-term
  ✦ Perfect for passive "set and forget" investors

3-FUND PORTFOLIO (sample)
  1. Total US Stock Market Index
  2. Total International Stock Index
  3. Total Bond Market Index

START SMALL
  • Many brokerages allow fractional shares from ₦1,000
  • Automate monthly contributions
  • Reinvest dividends to compound growth

COMPOUND EXAMPLE
  ₦50,000 invested each month for 40 years
  at 8% average annual return
  ≈ ₦150,000,000

BROKERAGES TO CONSIDER
  • Local banks with investment arms
  • International platforms supporting Nigerian users
  • Always check regulation and fee schedules
