UNDERSTANDING STUDENT LOANS
FinSmart – Money Confidence for Students
==================================================

LOAN TYPES
  Federal / Government    Lower rates, flexible repayment
  Private / Bank         Credit-based, fewer protections

KEY TERMS
  Principal      The amount you borrowed
  Interest       The cost of borrowing
  Capitalization Unpaid interest added to principal

REPAYMENT STRATEGIES
  ✦ Pay interest while still in school (if possible)
  ✦ Make extra payments toward principal
  ✦ Refinance only when you have stable income
  ✦ Look into income-driven repayment plans
  ✦ Avoid loan forbearance unless emergency

  Loan 2: ₦________  Rate: ___%  Min Payment: ₦________
  Loan 3: ₦________  Rate: ___%  Min Payment: ₦________

DEBT SNOWBALL VS AVALANCHE
  Snowball:  Pay smallest balance first
  Avalanche: Pay highest interest rate first
  Pick the method that keeps you motivated.
