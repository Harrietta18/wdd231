BORROWING WISELY AS A NIGERIAN STUDENT
FinSmart - Money Confidence for Nigerian Students
==================================================

COMMON BORROWING SOURCES
  Bank or microfinance loan
  Cooperative loan
  Loan app
  Family or friends
  School-related support

KEY TERMS
  Principal      The amount borrowed
  Interest       The cost of borrowing
  Fees           Extra charges beyond interest
  Due Date       The day repayment is expected
  Penalty        Extra cost for late payment

BEFORE YOU BORROW
  [ ] Write down the total repayment amount.
  [ ] Confirm the repayment date.
  [ ] Check all fees.
  [ ] Know what happens if you pay late.
  [ ] Make sure the loan is for a real need.

REPAYMENT STRATEGIES
  - Pay on or before the due date.
  - Pay the most expensive debt first if you can.
  - Avoid taking a new loan to repay an old one.
  - Talk early if you know you will be late.
  - Do not borrow for status or pressure spending.
