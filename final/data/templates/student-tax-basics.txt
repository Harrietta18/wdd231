DO STUDENTS PAY TAXES?
  In Nigeria:
    • Employment income is taxed under PAYE
    • Your employer deducts and remits automatically
    • Side-hustle / freelance income is self-assessed
    • Small income thresholds may apply

WHAT TO KEEP TRACK OF
  [ ] W-2 equivalent / payslips
  [ ] 1099 / invoices for freelance work
  [ ] Education-related receipts
  [ ] Bank statements (Dec/Jan)
  [ ] Investment income statements

STUDENT-FRIENDLY TIPS
  ✦ Open a separate bank account for business income
  ✦ Track deductible expenses (books, supplies, transport)
  ✦ File early — penalties add up
  ✦ Use free filing tools if your income is simple
  ✦ Keep digital copies of every receipt for 3 years

WHEN TO ASK A PROFESSIONAL
  • You earned income from 2+ sources
  • You started investing
  • You received a scholarship with conditions
  • You're unsure about deductions

KEY DATES (general guidance)
  Q1  Start gathering documents
  Q2  Submit returns / confirm PAYE
  Q3  Make any top-up payments
  Q4  Plan ahead for next year
