TAX AND INCOME BASICS FOR NIGERIAN STUDENTS
FinSmart - Money Confidence for Nigerian Students
==================================================

DO STUDENTS PAY TAXES?
Some students may have taxable income, especially from paid work, freelance services, or a small business.
Employment income may be handled through PAYE, while business or freelance income may require better records.

WHAT TO KEEP TRACK OF
  [ ] Payslips or payment messages.
  [ ] Invoices for freelance work.
  [ ] Receipts for business expenses.
  [ ] Bank statements.
  [ ] Investment income records.

STUDENT-FRIENDLY TIPS
  - Open a separate account or wallet for business income.
  - Track expenses like data, transport, tools, and supplies.
  - Keep digital copies of receipts.
  - Ask a qualified tax professional when income becomes regular.
  - Learn the rules before your side hustle grows.

WHEN TO ASK FOR HELP
  - You earn from more than one source.
  - You started a registered business.
  - You sell products or services regularly.
  - You are unsure what records to keep.
